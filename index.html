Life TV

A personal, fan-made app for watching every creator's point of view of the Minecraft Life Series, in one searchable place.

What it does

  • Plays public YouTube videos, sorted by season, episode and creator.
  • Remembers what you've watched and keeps your list of saved videos.
  • Runs on Windows, Android phones and Android TV.
  • Can keep your devices in step through a folder in your own Google Drive.

Who it's for

Life TV is a private project made for personal use. It isn't sold, and it has no accounts, adverts or tracking.

Privacy

Read the privacy policy to see what the app stores and where.

Life TV is an unofficial fan project. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube, Google, Mojang, Microsoft or any Life Series creator. All videos belong to their creators and play through YouTube.
