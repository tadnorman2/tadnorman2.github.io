Life TV Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Life TV is a personal, fan-made app. It has no accounts, adverts or analytics, and it has no server of its own. The person who makes Life TV doesn't receive your data.

What the app stores

On your own devices, Life TV stores your watch progress, saved videos, Watch Party picks, Moments Map times, settings, and a list of public YouTube videos.

Google Drive

If you choose to connect Google Drive, Life TV makes a "Life TV sync" folder in your own Drive and keeps two files there: your watch data and the video list. It uses the drive.file permission, so it can only reach files it created. It can't see anything else in your Drive.

Your sign-in is stored only on the device you connected. Google Drive data is used only to keep your own devices in step. It isn't sold, shared with anyone, or used for advertising.

YouTube

Videos play through YouTube's embedded player, and the video list comes from the YouTube Data API. YouTube's use of data is covered by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Home network sync

If you pair a phone or TV with Life TV on your PC, your data moves directly between those devices over your home network.

Removing your data

  • In the app, choose Disconnect this device under Settings, then Sync away from home.
  • Delete the "Life TV sync" folder from your Google Drive.
  • Remove Life TV's access at your Google Account permissions page.
  • Uninstall the app to remove what's stored on that device.
